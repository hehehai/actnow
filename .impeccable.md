## Design Context

### Users
Actnow is a reusable application starter for people adapting a content publishing site to their own project. Administrators create and publish content; visitors read it; signed-in users manage their profile.

### Brand Personality
Clear, warm, adaptable. Keep the product approachable and the content easy to scan.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use HSKRead as the visual reference: Google Sans Flex, warm paper surfaces, quiet borders, simple navigation, and restrained orange accents. Support both light and dark themes.

### Design Principles
- Keep starter-template features small and easy to replace.
- Make published content the primary focus.
- Keep content editing limited to title, description, cover, body, and publication status.
- Reserve content creation and publishing for administrators.
- Use the same visual language across public pages, account pages, and the admin workspace.
